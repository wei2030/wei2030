## 👨‍💻 About Me

Final-year **Computer Science (Data Engineering)** student at **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia** (CGPA 3.96), graduating July 2026, with a full year of enterprise data engineering experience at **PPG Coatings (Malaysia)** — a Fortune 500 manufacturer.

I work across the data lifecycle: designing and operating ETL/ELT pipelines in Azure Data Factory and Databricks, building Delta Lake architectures, and delivering Power BI analytics for business stakeholders. My final-year project delivered an automated document-processing pipeline that reduced processing time by **~75%** and cost by **~80%**, at **100%** extraction accuracy in testing.

🎯 Currently open to **Data Engineer** and **Data Analyst** roles.
