Final-year Computer Science (Data Engineering) student at **Universiti Teknologi Malaysia** (CGPA 3.96), graduating July 2026, with a full year of enterprise data engineering experience at **PPG Coatings (Malaysia)** — a Fortune 500 manufacturer.
